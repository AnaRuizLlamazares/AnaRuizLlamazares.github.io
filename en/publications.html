Artist of the Year — Ana Ruiz-Llamazares

Honoured at the Château de Belleville with the exhibition Angles de vie (16 June – 3 July). Frames, colours, light and the enigmatic figure of the “Passer-by” run through her canvases. Article published in the cultural bulletin of Gif-sur-Yvette.

Artists' Outing — The frames of Ana Ruiz Llamazares

Portrait of the artist in her studio opening onto the living room, on the occasion of the Artists' Festival (5–6 June 2004). “In my paintings I am interested in the question of the frame, the boundary, the dimensions. I think I like to make the viewer look differently.”

Artists' Festival & Artist of the Year

Programme of the Artists' Festival in Gif-sur-Yvette (Saturday 4 and Sunday 5 June 2004). Ana Ruiz Llamazares is named “Artist of the Year 2004–2005”. She will exhibit in June 2005 in the grand salon of the Château de Belleville.

Critical text · 2005

Angles of Life — Presentation text

“Ana Ruiz-Llamazares' Angles of Life often follow one another, sometimes multiply, but always answer each other. […] A true hymn to life, Ana Ruiz-Llamazares' painting is luminous, warm and deep.” Biography and list of exhibitions.
